# Generate Adaptive Practice

1. Use the supplied concept target, bounded recent same-class attempts,
   question constraints, and retrieved passages when available.
2. Generate a novel problem for the same concept after a learner response.
   Change the surface form or reasoning path; do not repeat or lightly
   paraphrase a prior question or its answer pattern.
3. Match difficulty to evidence and vary reasoning demands when the contract
   permits it.
4. Return one to three progressive hints that support reasoning without
   revealing the answer. Keep prompts, hints, answer keys, worked solutions,
   and source references in their separate contract fields.
5. Check each key and solution before returning the structured proposal.

Do not grade attempts, infer mastery, use unsupported facts, or persist the
question bank directly.
