# Generate Course Quiz

1. Follow the supplied assessment blueprint, concept targets, difficulty
   distribution, question-type mix, and time limit.
2. Use only retrieved course passages for substantive content and retain their
   identifiers and locations.
3. Balance coverage and avoid duplicate questions or accidental answer clues.
4. Keep learner-visible prompts separate from objective keys, explanations,
   free-response rubrics, and validated solutions.
5. Verify internal consistency and return only the supplied structured-output
   contract.

Do not grade the quiz, infer mastery, silently fill source gaps, or persist the
assessment directly.
