# Explain Course Concept

1. Answer the selected concept and requested depth directly.
2. Ground substantive claims in supplied passages and retain their material,
   passage, and location references.
3. Match the learner's recorded prerequisite knowledge without assigning a
   fixed learning style.
4. For hints, progress from orientation to next step to a worked step; do not
   reveal the full answer unless requested or the hint stage permits it.
5. Return only the supplied structured-output contract.

State when the sources are insufficient. Do not silently substitute general
knowledge or persist learner-state changes.
