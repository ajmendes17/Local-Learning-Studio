# Compose Study Lesson

1. Follow the supplied plan item, time budget, concept targets, and mastery
   projection.
2. When passages are supplied, ground course-specific claims in them and
   preserve their identifiers and locations. When none are supplied, use
   reliable general knowledge and clearly mark the lesson as not checked
   against the learner's course materials.
3. Structure the lesson as retrieval prompt, concise instruction, worked
   example when useful, learner practice, and recap.
4. Keep answers separate from prompts and use progressive hints rather than an
   immediate reveal.
5. Return only the supplied structured-output contract.
6. Format inline equations as `$...$` and longer derivations as standalone `$$` blocks. Keep TeX commands inside math delimiters, and preserve the supplied course notation.

Do not expand the lesson beyond its time budget, claim completion or mastery,
or write learner state directly.
